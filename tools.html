Conversions worth memorizing

  • km/h → m/s: divide by 3.6 (100 km/h ≈ 27.8 m/s)
  • 1 mi = 1609 m · 1 ft = 0.3048 m · 1 in = 2.54 cm
  • 1 kg weighs 9.8 N on Earth · 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb
  • Grams → kilograms before using F = ma: 250 g = 0.250 kg
  • Minutes and hours → seconds before using the Big 3

The rules

  1. Non-zero digits always count: 4.56 has 3.
  2. Zeros between non-zero digits count: 4005 has 4.
  3. Leading zeros never count: 0.0045 has 2.
  4. Trailing zeros count only if there's a decimal point: 45.00 has 4, 4500. has 4, but 4500 is ambiguous (assume 2).
  5. In scientific notation, every digit in the front number counts: 3.00 × 10⁸ has 3.
  6. × and ÷: answer gets the fewest sig figs of the inputs. + and −: answer gets the fewest decimal places.
  7. Don't round in the middle of a problem. Keep extra digits and round only the final answer (usually to 3 sig figs).
